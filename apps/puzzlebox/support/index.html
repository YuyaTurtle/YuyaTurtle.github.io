PuzzleBox Support

日本語

PuzzleBox is a box of logic puzzle games, with a new one featured each season and a daily puzzle from each game you've unlocked.

Restoring a purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings in PuzzleBox and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

What "Remove ads" does

It removes banner ads and the ads between levels, for good. "Watch to continue" on the fail screen is still offered, because it's optional: you only see that video if you choose to.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new phone

PuzzleBox keeps your progress on your device, with no account. Your progress moves to a new phone when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Purchases can always be restored as above.

Personalized ads

Whether ads may use your advertising identifier is up to you. Change it in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or from the link in PuzzleBox's Settings.
