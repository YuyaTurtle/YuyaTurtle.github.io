PuzzleBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

日本語

PuzzleBox is a free puzzle game paid for by ads. We have no account system and no server, and we don't collect your data ourselves. The ads are served by Google, and Google does collect some information from your device to do that. This page explains both parts.

What we store, and where

Your progress (levels, coins, hearts, badges, items) and your settings are stored on your device only. We never receive a copy, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Your device backups include this data, the same way they include other apps' data. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Ads, and what Google collects

PuzzleBox shows ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. To choose and measure ads, prevent fraud and report on performance, the SDK sends Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below)
  • other device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the SDK

Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We don't receive this information about you personally. AdMob gives us only totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Tracking permission

After you've played the first few levels, PuzzleBox asks once, through Apple's standard prompt, whether it may track you. If you allow it, Google can use your advertising identifier to show ads more relevant to you. If you don't, you still see ads, but they aren't based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. The game is the same either way.

You can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or from the link in PuzzleBox's Settings.

Purchases

Purchases such as "Remove ads" are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

"Remove ads" removes banner ads and ads between levels. Optional reward videos, which you choose to watch, stay available, so the Google Mobile Ads SDK remains part of the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting PuzzleBox removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To ask Google about its data, use the options in Google's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

PuzzleBox is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app tells Google that its ads are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If PuzzleBox ever changes what happens to your data, for example by adding an account or online features, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
