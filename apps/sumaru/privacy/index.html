Sumaru Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Sumaru is an ADHD-friendly task app built around one rule: your data stays yours. Sumaru does not run its own servers and does not collect, store, or have access to your task data. This page explains exactly what that means.

What's stored, and where

Everything you create in Sumaru — tasks, steps, categories, completion history, settings — is stored locally on your device in a private on-device database. Sumaru never transmits this data to any server operated by us, because no such server exists.

If you turn on cross-device sync:

  • iOS syncs through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), the same way Apple's own apps sync your data. We never see it — it stays inside your Apple account, governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Android syncs to a folder you choose yourself (via Android's built-in file picker) — for example a folder inside your own Google Drive or other cloud storage. Sumaru only writes to that folder; it never has its own copy or access to your wider account.

Uninstalling Sumaru deletes the local copy of your data. Anything synced to your own iCloud or chosen folder remains entirely under your control, managed through that service's own settings — not through us.

Advertising

Sumaru shows banner ads (never during an active task timer) served by Google AdMob. Google's ad SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, per Google's Privacy Policy and its AdMob data disclosure.

Every time you open Sumaru, it checks whether a consent form is required under Google's User Messaging Platform (the standard GDPR/UMP consent flow) before any ad is requested — a form is only shown when your region and settings require one.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device's own operating system. They are never sent through a push-notification server, because Sumaru doesn't have one.

Children's privacy

Sumaru is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If what Sumaru collects or how it's used ever changes — for example, adding privacy-respecting crash reporting — this page will be updated first, with the "last updated" date above reflecting the change.
