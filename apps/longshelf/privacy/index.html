Longshelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

日本語

Longshelf collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no server. What you put in the box stays on your device. This page explains what that means in practice.

What's stored, and where

Everything you add to Longshelf (names, prices, links, pictures, notes, dates, your decisions) and your settings are stored on your device only. We never receive a copy, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Your device backups include this data, the same way they include other apps' data. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Links

When you add a link, Longshelf loads that web page from your device to fill in the name and a picture, the same way a browser would. The request goes directly from your device to that website. We don't see it. As with any page you visit, the website itself can see a normal web request from your device.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device and never pass through a server. Notifications and widgets never show an item's name or picture. Widgets show only counts and totals.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Exporting and deleting your data

If you export your data, the file goes only where you choose to send it. You can delete any item in the app at any time. Deleting Longshelf removes all of its data from your device; copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings.

Children's privacy

Longshelf collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Longshelf ever changes what it does with your data, for example by adding sync through your own iCloud account, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
