Longshelf Support

日本語

Longshelf is a closed box for things you want. Put something away, and decide about it when its wait is over.

Restoring a purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the purchase screen (for example from History in Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Canceling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Longshelf. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new phone

Longshelf keeps everything on your device, with no account. Your box moves to a new phone when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also export your data as a CSV file from Settings.

Not getting notifications

Check that notifications for Longshelf are allowed in your iPhone's Settings. You can change the time of day they arrive in Longshelf's Settings. Even with notifications off, anything ready for a decision is waiting for you in the app.
